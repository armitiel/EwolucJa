# Onboarding — tło powitalne

Tryb: wbudowany ImageGen, `stylized-concept`.

Finalny asset używany w aplikacji: `frontend/public/assets/onboarding/tlo-powitanie-v2.webp`.

## Finalny prompt

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: full-screen onboarding background for the EwolucJA children’s educational adventure game

Primary request: Create a brand-new magical forest background that visually belongs to the same game as the supplied start screen, replacing the dark forest currently behind the onboarding form.

Input images: Image 1 is the current onboarding screen and defines the overlay-safe composition; Image 2 is the approved start screen and defines the polished art direction, lighting, palette, and world.

Scene/backdrop: bright enchanted woodland clearing with a welcoming golden path, softly modeled fir and birch trees framing the far left and right edges, small flowers and mushrooms near the lower edges, a few delicate golden and purple magical motes, blue-turquoise sky and warm morning sunlight.

Subject: environment only; no characters.

Style/medium: premium polished soft 3D children’s-game illustration, rounded friendly forms, rich but gentle detail, matching the fox and purple wizard world from the reference.

Composition/framing: wide landscape 3:2 to 16:10 full-screen background. Keep the entire central vertical 45% calm, luminous, and low-detail so the wizard and cream form card can sit there. Preserve a useful center crop for tall mobile screens: the centered crop must still read as a forest clearing and must not cut through a large tree trunk. Place richer foliage mostly around the outer edges. Leave the upper corners slightly deeper in tone so small navigation and progress controls remain readable.

Lighting/mood: optimistic morning adventure, warm sunbeams, gentle magical welcome; much brighter and friendlier than the current dark background.

Color palette: turquoise sky, fresh emerald and moss green, warm gold and honey, small purple accents; harmonious with the approved start screen.

Text: none.

Constraints: background only; no fox, no wizard, no children, no silhouettes, no UI, no cards, no buttons, no border, no logo, no text, no watermark. The center must remain visually quiet behind the onboarding content.

Avoid: dark horror forest, black foreground trees, flat vector look, painterly watercolor, photorealism, excessive bloom, large foreground objects in the center, busy central details.

Finalnie nie używają generowanych portretów. Są prostymi, edytowalnymi SVG w `frontend/src/pages/Onboarding.jsx`, złożonymi z dużych kształtów i kolorów obecnych w grze.
